YouTube · Long form

Acting in Korea

A 9-minute, first-hand account of working as a foreign actor in Korea — written, presented and edited to hold attention without hype.

I worked as an actor in Korea for six months, starting with almost no experience, no Korean language and no connections. This video is my honest, realistic breakdown of what that is actually like — without illusions, hype or fake success stories.

It covers how the system really works: money and payment timelines, documents and visas, who you are genuinely competing with, how realistic acting with no experience is, and how Korea compares with Thailand, China and India. It closes on burnout and career change — why I moved into acting full time.

A self-produced format: research, script, on-camera presenting, shooting, editing and retention-focused pacing, all handled solo.
